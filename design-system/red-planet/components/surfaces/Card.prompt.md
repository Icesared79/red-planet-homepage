A grouped block of content set apart by tone rather than shadow.

```jsx
<Card tone="sunken"><Metric size="xl" label="Verified records" value="502,392,645" /></Card>
<Card tone="forest">…</Card>
```

- Red Planet: hero metric card, process cards, "How a new market is added".
- Atlas: prefer Panel (it has a header row); use Card for KPI tiles.
