§ 03

What a Customer Licenses

Every license is the data for a set of counties plus the seats a team needs. The views that apply to those counties come with it.

Data

Required
  • Priced per county, based on many factors
  • Same price whether the county is built yet or not
  • Can be licensed on its own

Views

Included
  • AtlasLand, AtlasCRE, AtlasProp and AtlasTrade, each where it applies to a licensed county
  • Included with the license at no charge
  • Cost built into the license, never charged per seat

Seats

  • Organizations buy blocks of 10
  • Individuals buy a single seat, at a higher price per seat
  • Each seat is tied to a company email

One annual price covers everything, with a 12-month minimum paid up front.

§ 04

The Customer Process

We build every license with the customer, starting with the data they need. They only pay once they've signed.

  1. 1

    Understand the Need

    We agree on the counties, data and user access with the customer.

  2. 2

    Build the Data Package

    We show how built counties are used today, or turn on the counties they need.

  3. 3

    Price the License

    Atlas prices each license by county and seats.

  4. 4

    Sign and Pay

    A 12-month minimum license fee is paid up front.

  5. 5

    Go Live and Renew

    Their access goes live in a few days to weeks, and the license renews each year once they are fully online.

§ 05

The Same Terms for Every Customer

Residential or commercial, one seat or hundreds, every agreement follows the same four rules.

Ownership

Red Planet Data owns all data

Customers license the data, never own it. Every agreement is signed with Red Planet Data, Inc.

Term

12-month minimum, paid up front

The license renews each year, and customers can add counties or seats at any time.

Seats

One company email per seat

Seats can be shared or charged for inside the organization, but never resold outside it.

Coverage

Almost any county, live in days to weeks

Florida and Connecticut are deepest today. Every other county is priced the same way.
