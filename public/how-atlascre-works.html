§ 01

What AtlasCRE is for.

AtlasCRE is the county record read for commercial property: the parcels, the owners behind them, the sales, and the deal pipeline of loans, liens, court filings and permits as each is recorded.

What it reads

  • Commercial parcelsevery commercial property on the roll, with its use and its value
  • Ownersthe companies that hold them and the people behind those companies, grouped
  • Saleseach recorded sale with its price, the day the clerk records it
  • The deal pipelineloans, liens, court filings and permits as they are recorded

Who uses it

  • Lenderswho is borrowing, against what, and what is going into default
  • Investors and developerswhat traded, for how much, and who is assembling
  • Brokers and REITsowners with activity on more than one property

§ 03

License and terms.

AtlasCRE comes with the license for any county where Atlas holds the commercial record, at no extra charge.

  • The county record

    Every record Atlas holds for the county comes with the license.

  • Every view that applies

    This view and the other three, each where it applies, at no extra charge.

  • Seats

    In blocks of 10 for an organization, or one seat for an individual.
