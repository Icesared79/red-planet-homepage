§ 02

What is moving.

Population grew 17.7 percent over five years, among the fastest in the country, and net domestic migration reached 3,747 people in 2025. Tax-return data show 2,349 net incoming individuals per 100,000 residents in 2023 and a net income gain of $935 per resident, or $153.7M in all.

§ 08

How the record is built.

  1. Intake. One process brings any county online: a registry of every board that decides land, readers for 14 meeting platforms, and a nightly watch that records when each clerk posts.
  2. Local reading. Scanned documents and meeting recordings are read by Red Planet Data’s own OCR and speech-to-text, on its own machines.
  3. Cases with proof. Every value in a case is tied to the document, page and passage it came from, and a field is shown only after it clears an accuracy bar against a hand-read reference set.
  4. The owners behind the owners. Parcels are linked to the companies that hold them and the people behind those companies.
  5. Assembly detection. Adjacent parcels bought by related entities within a window are flagged as a possible assembly.
  6. The County Catalog. Every US county profiled and scored, with a record of what can be obtained there and how fast.
  7. Coverage and lag. Every county states what is captured and how quickly, measured.
§ 09

Intake and terms.

Every county goes through the same five steps, so a new county is online in days to weeks.

  1. 1

    Map the county

    Every board that decides land, in the county and its towns.

  2. 2

    Collect the record

    Several years of agendas, packets and minutes, plus the land records.

  3. 3

    Read it into cases

    Each value tied to its document and page.

  4. 4

    Add what is coming

    Road funding, capacity decisions, annexations, fee studies, rule changes.

  5. 5

    State the coverage

    Which boards are in, from when, and what is not available.

Per county, same price built or not.

Seats in blocks of 10, or one seat for an individual.

Twelve months, paid in advance, renewing annually.

First 30 days of a new county included in full, agreement and price stated on day one.
